# SECTION_TEMPLATES.md
# Fill-in-the-Blank Section Prompts
# Copy the template for the section you want, fill in [BRACKETS], paste to AI.

---

## HOW TO USE

1. Copy the full template block
2. Fill every [BRACKET] with your creative direction
3. Delete any lines that don't apply
4. Paste to AI after your MASTER_PROMPT.md

---
