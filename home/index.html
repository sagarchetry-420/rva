PRINCIPAL'S MESSAGE

"Welcome to the
Phrontistery of Knowledge
and Harmony."

I am happy that you are applying for admission in our institute and would like to thank you for trusting us and for giving a golden opportunity to be a part in building the career of your child

We believe, there is never any point in life of a person when we can sit back and say that we had learnt enough. In the words of Carl Sagan, “Somewhere, something incredible is waiting to be known”.

Education is not just about the subjects that are learnt and taught in school. Becoming educated is not restricted to being in school and then in college, gathering certificates. It is a life-long exercise that can be unbelievably exciting if only we wish to jump onto the train of experience and take a trip to every conceivable place on the earth. So, we are here to encourage children to ask as many questions as they can arise and learn as much as possible. On behalf of my enthusiastic team, I can assure and give words that your child will get the real world skills which will help them to succeed in today’s exceedingly competitive and challenging world.

Our school was established in the year 2008, and we started with only 52 students with all required infrastructural facilities. Now we are proud to announce that in the academic session 2025-26 we have 750+ students’ strength and 40+ staff. And also would like to let you know that from the academic session 2022-23 we have started the Senior Secondary class of the Arts Stream.

Bhargav Chetia

Principal, Rose Valley Academy

M.Sc. (Phy), M.A. (Edu ), B.Ed, TET qualified
